**Project Name:**  Study Buddy
**Project Type:**  Flutter Android Application

**Study Buddy** is a Flutter-based Android application designed to help students manage their academic activities in one place. The app provides useful features such as timetable management, assignment tracking, notes, exam countdowns, Pomodoro focus mode, and study streak tracking. The main purpose of the app is to improve student productivity, time management, and study consistency through a simple and user-friendly mobile interface.

**Introduction**

Students often need to manage classes, assignments, exams, notes, and study together. Using different apps or manual methods can be difficult and confusing. Study Buddy provides a simple solution by combining important student productivity tools into an Android application.
The app is designed with a clean interface so students can quickly view their schedule, track tasks, save notes, prepare for exams, and stay focused during study sessions.

Problem and Solution

****Problem:****

•	Students often forget assignment deadlines.

•	Class schedules are difficult to manage manually.

•	Notes may be scattered in different places.

•	Students may lack focus during study time.

•	Exam preparation is harder without countdown reminders.

****Solution:****

Study Buddy solves these problems by providing a single mobile app where students can manage their timetable, tasks, notes, exams, focus sessions, and study streaks.
